I entered my first hackathon alone, at HackYeah, mostly to find out whether I
could. What I actually learned there had nothing to do with typing speed, and
every event since — an EEG classifier at Neurohackathon, a first place in
Energy & Offshore, a four-stage vision pipeline at Hackology — has taught the
same lesson in a different costume.
