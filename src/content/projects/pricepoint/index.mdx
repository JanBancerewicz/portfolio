Most of what I have written about elsewhere on this site is research or a
hackathon — work where the worst outcome is a disappointing number. PricePoint
is the other kind. It is the premium calculation system at ERGO Hestia, and it
answers over **200,000 pricing requests a day**. A wrong answer there is not a
failing test; it is a policy priced incorrectly.

## What I do on it

Develop and maintain the system as a Data & Software Engineering Intern —

## What production taught me that university did not

- **Correctness has a blast radius.** The same bug is trivial in a lab and
  expensive at 200k requests a day, and that difference is entirely about
  context, not about the code.
- **Boring is a feature.** The parts of this system I trust most are the parts
  nobody has felt the need to be clever about.
- **Observability before optimisation.** You cannot make a claim about
  performance on a system you cannot see into.
